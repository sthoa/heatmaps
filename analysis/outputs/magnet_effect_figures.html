Methods & limits

  • Band depth: photos were perspective-corrected to the 10×10 mm block, intensity-normalized against the white holder in each frame, and the NP band measured as the furthest point (from the gap edge) where darkening exceeds the block’s own base tint by 15 L*.
  • What is resolvable: the high-contrast band near the gap. A diffuse interior tint attributable to the magnet could not be separated from imaging noise — phone auto-exposure varies ±30 L* between shots, the magnet casts a shadow on its side of the block, and ambient light drifted over the 21.5 h run.
  • Wall arrival (magnet arms): after removing the magnets, a dark NP patch is visible by eye at the block wall on the magnet side. Consistent with this, the wall-interface zone in magnet-arm photos darkens progressively (−9 L* at 1 h → −26 L* at 21.5 h vs the same block at t = 0 with magnet attached), while the gap zone depletes faster than in controls — NPs crossed to the wall through the agarose (on disassembly the deposit sits directly in front of the magnet face, not at the bottom or side seams) — a dilute through-gel flux, invisible in transit, concentrating at the wall — alongside the slow bulk band (≤4 mm). Caveat: the magnet’s shadow occupies the same pixels and responds to the large ambient-light changes over the run (day–night–day), so the deposit cannot be quantified per condition from these images; a dense deposit reads near-black (not brown), so color cannot separate the two either.
  • Statistics: paired across the 8 conditions, the magnet adds on average +0.44 mm at 21.5 h (t = 2.66, 7/8 conditions positive). Differences between coatings, BSA, and agarose concentrations are within repeat-level noise.
  • To quantify the wall deposit: photograph all blocks magnet-off (top and side views) at the end of a run — the pipeline can then measure far-wall accumulation per condition without occlusion.
  • For the next run: lock exposure/white balance (Pro mode), fix the phone on a mount, keep a gray card in frame, and consider a destructive endpoint (gel sectioning + ICP or UV-Vis) to quantify interior NP content that top-view color cannot see.
